## Voice

Plain and unshowy. They describe themselves as *"אנחנו אופים, לא מעצבים"*. Two people who get
up at four in the morning. Write like that — no marketing gloss, no superlatives, no claims
about being the best. They didn't ask for it and it would read as false.
